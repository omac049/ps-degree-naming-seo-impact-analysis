## Key Findings

### Search Demand Gap
- Current program names generate **~13,910 monthly searches** — proposed names total just **50 combined**
- "MA in Psychology online" alone drives 5,400/mo; proposed MPS equivalent: **0 searches**

### Designation Market Gap
- **"Bachelor of Arts degree"** = 40,500/mo vs **"Bachelor of Professional Studies"** = 390/mo (104x less)
- Approved BA renames **keep Arts designation** (favorable for SEO)
- MPS-CH is Professional Studies — treat designation as supplemental brand language; build launch SEO around Community Health + MPS terminology without claiming MPH equivalence

### Strategic Recommendation
1. Dual-language SEO for the **4 BA renames** (go-live 10/27/2026)
2. Net-new MPS-CH page, content, and campaigns (go-live 11/3/2026) — **no “formerly”**
3. Separate MPH and Sociology sunset/redirect plans
4. Hold Carnegie / paused / ECD SEO asset changes
5. Publish Group B Wave 1 with Applied definition framework; hold Wave 2
