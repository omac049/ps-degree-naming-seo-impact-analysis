## Key Findings

### Search Demand Gap
- Current program names generate **~13,910 monthly searches** — proposed names total just **50 combined**
- "MA in Psychology online" alone drives 5,400/mo; proposed MPS equivalent: **0 searches**

### Designation Market Gap
- **"Bachelor of Arts degree"** = 40,500/mo vs **"Bachelor of Professional Studies"** = 390/mo (104x less)
- **"Bachelor of Science degree"** = 60,500/mo vs **"Bachelor of Professional Studies"** = 390/mo (155x less)
- **"Master of Science degree"** = 12,100/mo vs **"Master of Professional Studies"** = 1,600/mo (8x less)
- **"BPS degree online"** and **"MPS degree online"** = **0 monthly searches**
- Approved BA renames **keep Arts designation** (favorable for SEO). MPH → Professional Studies still needs dual-language mitigation.

### Enrollment Context
- These programs generated **56,317 leads** and **832 enrollments** (Jul 2025 – Jun 2026)
- Graduate decisions grew **+15% YoY** under current names

### Strategic Recommendation
Execute SEO mitigation for the **6 approved** renames now (preserve URLs, dual-language naming). Hold SEO asset changes for Carnegie-held and paused programs. Lean into Arts/Science market demand where designation stays unchanged.

## Strategic Recommendations (Summary)

1. **Preserve All Existing URLs** — non-negotiable for approved renames
2. **Implement Dual-Language Naming on Every Approved Page** — title tags, H1s, meta descriptions
3. **Lean Into Arts Designation Keywords** — approved BA renames keep Arts; don't abandon that search market
4. **Prioritize SEO work for the 6 approved programs** — hold assets for Carnegie / paused
5. **Establish Rollback Criteria** — define thresholds before any rename goes live
6. **Monitor GSC + Enrollment Weekly for 6 Months** — cross-reference search and funnel data
7. **Build "What Is Professional Studies?" Hub Content** — especially for MPH → Community Health
